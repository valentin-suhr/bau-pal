# Data, confidence and limitations

## Main sources

- Berlin ALKIS parcel and building geometry.
- Berlin official B-Plan scopes and documentation records.
- Reviewed B-Plan sheets and text stipulations.
- Berlin Baunutzungsplan, Fluchtlinien and FNP context for parcels outside a
  resolved controlling B-Plan.
- QGIS-derived Lichterfelde residential, street, park and public-space masks.

Source URLs, retrieval metadata and evidence locators are retained by the
normalised processing model where available. The deployable demo contains only
the bounded records required for Lichterfelde.

## What the indicators mean

- **Vacant candidate:** no qualifying building evidence after land-use
  screening. This is not proof that land is for sale or immediately buildable.
- **Underutilised candidate:** estimated existing development is below an
  established legal density value. The estimate may omit constraints that are
  not yet modelled.
- **Purple point:** both GRZ and GFZ are populated with supporting planning
  evidence. It does not imply that building form, envelope, height, heritage,
  trees, easements, parking or authority review are complete.
- **Indicative value:** a transparent demo assumption, not a market appraisal
  or machine-learned production valuation.

## Publication caveats

The project is a research and screening prototype. Users must verify current
planning law, legal effect, parcel boundaries and site constraints with the
responsible Berlin authority and qualified professionals before making a land,
design or investment decision.

Before publishing a live service, confirm the current terms of use and required
attribution for every redistributed geodata layer.
